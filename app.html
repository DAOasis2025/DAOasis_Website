The Living Ecosystem

Six metrics.
One living world.

Everything you track shapes the environment around you.

Steps
The Journey
Your daily movement becomes distance on the map. Every step carries you further toward your destination.
Breathing
The Weather
Consistent breathwork clears the sky. Miss sessions and the atmosphere shifts — subtle, never punishing.
Hydration
The River
Each water log feeds a river in your app. Full days make it flow. The river never disappears — it waits.
Sleep
The Night Sky
Quality sleep builds constellations. A 30-day streak fills the sky. Stars never disappear — they simply stop growing.
Nutrition
The Garden
Log nourishment — not calories. A varied diet grows a garden. It responds to everything else you do.
Streaks
The Path Behind You
See how far you have come, not just how far you have left. Progress stays. The path is always there.

The Community

The Sanctuary

The journey leads
somewhere real.
