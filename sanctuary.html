A rolling programme — there is no fixed cohort start date. New members arrive every day, entering a community already in motion rather than starting from zero. You arrive. You join the rhythm. You begin where the programme currently is.

Each day builds on the last. Recovery creates the space for learning. Learning deepens accountability. Accountability strengthens community. By day seven, you are not simply leaving a retreat — you are leaving with a different relationship to your own behaviour.

Sanctuary Life

What the days are actually made of.

01 — Movement

Move because it feels good,not because it is scheduled.

Walking, mobility, breath and open water. Movement at the Sanctuary is built around what the body actually responds to, not around filling a timetable.

02 — Recovery

Sleep treated as the foundation,not the afterthought.

Light, temperature, rhythm and evening quiet are designed into the environment. Recovery is not a session you attend — it is the condition everything else runs on.

03 — Learning

Frameworks you canuse on Monday.

Small workshops, practical and applied. The measure of a session is not how it felt in the room — it is whether it still works three weeks later at home.

04 — Nature & Reflection

The environmentdoes part of the work.

Open air, water, distance and time without a screen. Some of the most useful hours of the week are the ones with nothing scheduled in them at all.

05 — Community

You arrive alone.You do not stay that way.

Shared meals, shared practice and honest conversation with people doing the same work. Accountability becomes far easier when it is not a private project.

The Point of It

You do not come here to escape your life.

You come here to return to it differently.

The Sanctuary is not designed to become another place to hide. It is designed to create the space, awareness and habits that can travel home with you — where sleep, movement, breath and attention go on compounding, one ordinary day at a time.
