03  ·  The Companion App

Every healthy habit moves you forward.

The Companion App is the everyday part of DAOasis — the place your routine lives. Instead of separate scores and streaks, everything you do moves one marker along a real route.

Six daily habits
Move, Rest, Breathe, Hydrate, Nourish and Learn, on one screen. Each one feeds the same journey.
A real road
The launch Journey runs 900 km from Bangkok to Phuket, through Ayutthaya, Hua Hin, Surat Thani and Khao Lak. Your progress is distance you can see.
Habits that help each other
A good night’s sleep carries tomorrow’s walk further. Consistency compounds instead of resetting.
Learning built in
Twenty short lessons in the pilot, ten in the Wellness Track and ten in the Web3 Track.
Recognition for showing up
Consistency earns DAOasis Reward Credits (DRC), a record of your participation held inside the app.
 

It works with the devices you already wear: Apple Health and Google Health Connect first, with more integrations planned. You do not need to understand crypto to use any of it.

The app is in build. Free to join when early access opens.
