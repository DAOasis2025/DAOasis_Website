04  ·  The Sanctuary

Somewhere real to reset.

The Companion App lives inside your day. The Sanctuary is where you step out of it.

We are testing the Sanctuary first with a hospitality partner in Thailand, before any property is taken on, and no date is set. It is built around a rolling seven-day rhythm. There is no fixed start date — you arrive and join the rhythm already in motion.

Seven days
Arrive. Recover. Learn. Practise. Account. Connect. Integrate.
Four foundations
Recovery, learning, accountability and community — one system, practised in person.
Something to take home
The habits you build there carry straight back into the app and your everyday routine.
 

The Sanctuary is optional: DAOasis works fully without it. Stays will be booked and paid in ordinary currency, and the credits you earn in the app are designed to contribute toward one.

The location and programme are still in development. As an early access member, you will hear first when expressions of interest open.
