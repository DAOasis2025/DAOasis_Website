You are in. Welcome to DAOasis.

Thank you for joining the early access list. You have stepped into something we are building with real care: a wellness ecosystem designed around the way people actually live — busy, stretched, and rarely given the structure to look after themselves well.

DAOasis brings three parts together:

The Companion App
Everyday habits — movement, sleep, breathing, hydration and learning — that move you along real routes, starting with Bangkok to Phuket.
The Sanctuary
An optional, immersive place to step away and reset, coming to Phuket in 2027.
A community that grows with you
People building the same habits, and over time, a say in how DAOasis develops.
 

Over the next ten days we will send you four short notes: why we are building this, and how each part works. Nothing you did not ask for, and every email has a one-click unsubscribe.

The Companion App is in build today. When early access opens, you will be among the first to hear.
