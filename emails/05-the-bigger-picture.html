05  ·  The bigger picture

Wellness apps create users. DAOasis creates stakeholders.

Everything so far has been about you: your habits, your journey, your reset. The last piece is about what happens when many people do that together.

Community
Quests and streaks are better shared. Inside the app you will find people building the same habits, at the same pace.
Reward Credits
DRC recognise consistency and participation. They are earned, not bought, and live inside the app.
$DVT, optional
DRC are designed to convert into $DVT, the on-chain layer, for those who want to go further. The rate and eligibility are not set yet, and it is never required.
A say, over time
Governance will be introduced in stages, from founders-led today towards a community that helps decide what DAOasis becomes.
 

That is the arc we are building: from user, to participant, to contributor, to stakeholder. You do not need to understand crypto to take part in any of it, and you can stay with the app and the habits for as long as you like.

DAOasis is pre-launch. Nothing in this email is an offer or an investment, and no returns are promised.
