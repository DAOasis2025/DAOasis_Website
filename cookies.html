Most cookie policies are long because there is a lot to disclose. This one is short for the opposite reason.

01

The short answer

As at 17 August 2026

This website sets no cookies. It contains no analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising tags and no third-party measurement scripts of any kind. Nothing on it profiles you, follows you between sites, or reports your visit to anyone.

One item is stored in your browser's local storage: a single value recording whether you chose the light or dark appearance. It never leaves your device.

We would rather say this plainly than pad it out. If that changes — and it may, since a product in development eventually needs to understand how it is used — this page will be updated before anything goes live, and consent will be requested first where the law requires it.

02

What cookies are

A cookie is a small text file a website asks your browser to store, and which the browser sends back on later visits. It is how a site recognises that two visits came from the same browser.

Cookies are not the only mechanism of this kind, and a policy that only mentioned cookies would be misleading. The related technologies:

  • Local storage — data kept in your browser that, unlike a cookie, is not automatically sent to the server. This is the only mechanism this site uses.
  • Session storage — the same idea, but cleared when you close the tab.
  • Pixels and beacons — a tiny invisible image or script whose only purpose is to report that you loaded a page. None here.
  • Device and browser fingerprinting — identifying a visitor from the characteristics of their browser rather than from stored data. Not used, and not planned.
  • Software development kits (SDKs) — the equivalent inside a mobile app. Relevant to the Companion App when it is released, not to this website.

A first-party cookie is set by the site you are visiting. A third-party cookie is set by another company whose code the site has embedded — and it is third-party cookies that do most of the cross-site tracking people object to.

Two things that are not us

Web fonts. This site loads its typefaces — Cormorant Garamond and Frank Ruhl Libre — from Google Fonts. That request goes to Google's servers and Google may log it as an ordinary server request. No cookie is set by it, and it is not used to track you across sites. It is a genuine third-party request, so it is disclosed here rather than passed over.

Hosting. Serving a web page requires a server to receive the request. Our hosting provider may process ordinary request information — such as IP address and the page requested — in order to deliver the site and keep it secure. That is infrastructure rather than tracking, but it is processing, so it is stated.

05

Third-party cookies

No third party sets a cookie through this website. There is no embedded video player, no social widget, no chat widget, no advertising network and no analytics provider in it.

The one third-party request the site makes is for its web fonts, described in section 03.

If you follow a link from DAOasis to another company's website, that site's own cookies and policies apply from the moment you arrive. We have no control over them, and no visibility of them.

06

Managing cookies

Since this site sets no cookies, there is nothing here to refuse. The controls below matter for the web generally, and will matter here if this page ever changes.

Clearing the one thing we do store

To remove the appearance preference, clear site data for this website in your browser settings, or use your browser's private browsing mode, in which nothing persists after you close the window. Removing it simply means the site opens in its default dark appearance next time.

Browser controls

Every major browser lets you view and delete stored data, block third-party cookies, block all cookies, and clear everything when you close it. These settings are found under Privacy, Security or Site settings, depending on the browser. Because those menus move between versions, we would rather point you to your browser's own current help pages than print instructions here that will be wrong within a year.

Blocking all cookies and local storage will break many websites. On this one, the only effect is that your appearance choice is forgotten.

Wider controls

Your browser may also offer a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal. This site collects nothing to honour or ignore, so those signals make no difference here — but they are worth having switched on generally.

07

Consent

In many jurisdictions — including the UK and the EU — a website must obtain consent before setting anything that is not strictly necessary, and must make refusing as easy as accepting.

This website does not currently display a cookie banner, and does not currently need one, because it sets no cookies and runs no analytics. The single stored item is a preference you set yourself by using the appearance control, which is the kind of thing consent requirements are designed to permit.

There is no consent management platform on this site. That is an accurate reflection of there being nothing to consent to, rather than a gap. If analytics or any non-essential technology is introduced, a compliant consent mechanism will be implemented before it goes live — with genuine choice, refusal as easy as acceptance, and the ability to change your mind later. In plain terms

No banner, because there is nothing to ask you about. When there is, you will be asked properly.
