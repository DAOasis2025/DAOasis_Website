What to expect

Being straight about this is more useful than a promise nobody keeps.

One address, not six
Open
Every route on this page reaches info@daoasis.xyz. DAOasis is a small team, and one address that is read is more useful than six that are not. Say which of the routes above you are writing about in the subject line and it will reach the right person. Dedicated addresses will be published here as they are created.
Nothing on this website posts to a server
By design
DAOasis has no form-handling backend, and a form that silently discarded your message would be worse than no form at all. So every address here is an ordinary email link. The investor request panel looks like a form but is not one — it lays out your request and hands it to your own email client to send, which is why it says the client "should have opened" rather than claiming anything was received. The Sanctuary early access list states on submission that nothing is sent or stored, because that is true.
Response times
A target response time is Response time commitment to be confirmed. We would rather publish a commitment we can keep than a reassuring number we cannot. Privacy requests will be answered within the period the applicable law requires.
What not to send
Please do not send medical records, clinical information, or your wallet's private key or recovery phrase — nobody from DAOasis will ever ask for the latter, and any message that does is fraudulent. For a privacy request, send only what is needed to identify your account.
