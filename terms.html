08

Quests and participation

Quests are participation activities across five categories — Rest, Move, Learn, Reflect and Community. Completing them moves your journey forward and is recognised through DRC.

Verification is tiered according to how confirmable an action genuinely is: automatically verified through a connected platform, manually logged by you, or recorded on the honour system, with recognition weighted accordingly.

You agree to log participation honestly. Falsifying activity — by manipulating device data, using automation or simulation, or logging activity that did not happen — is a breach of these terms and may result in adjustment of your record, loss of DRC, or suspension. This is not a technicality: fabricated participation devalues the recognition earned by people who did the work.

DAOasis may adjust, correct or reverse participation records and DRC where they resulted from error, a technical fault, or a breach of these terms.

The design of quests, categories, journey mechanics, verification tiers and recognition weightings may change as the product develops.

09

DAOasis Reward Credits (DRC)

DRC recognises participation inside the DAOasis experience. It is earned through participation and held inside the app.

What DRC is not

DRC is not money, legal tender, electronic money, a deposit, a bank balance, a payment instrument, a financial product, a security or an investment. It has no monetary value guaranteed by DAOasis, is not redeemable for cash, and confers no ownership interest in DAOasis or any claim against it.

DRC exists to recognise participation. It should not be acquired, held or treated as something to speculate on.

DRC is personal to your account and may not be sold, transferred or traded except through a facility DAOasis expressly provides.

DRC may be convertible into $DVT. Conversion rates, caps, eligibility criteria and timing are In design and are subject to change, to implementation, and to applicable legal and regulatory requirements. DAOasis does not guarantee that conversion will be available to you, in your jurisdiction, at any particular rate, or at all.

DAOasis may modify how DRC is earned, weighted, capped or expired as the product develops. Where a change materially reduces DRC already recognised, we will give notice before it takes effect. DRC associated with an account ends when that account is closed or terminated.
