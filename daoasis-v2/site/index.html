The DAOasis ecosystem

One ecosystem.Three connected experiences.

DAOasis combines a daily wellness app, an optional real-world Sanctuary and digital ownership into one ongoing ecosystem. Every habit moves your Journey — across Thailand, up to Everest Base Camp and beyond — and completing one opens the next.
Built for founders, operators, creators and people working location-independently — demanding work, and little structure around it.

Companion App

The core operating environment of DAOasis — the daily relationship, and the foundation every later part of the ecosystem reaches you through. Rituals, learning and quests are woven into your routine, so consistency builds naturally over time.

What membership means

A community thatcompounds — together.

Most wellness platforms treat community as a feature. DAOasis treats it as the architecture. When you build habits alongside others, accountability becomes structural — not motivational.

Community members don't just participate in the DAOasis ecosystem — they help shape its direction. Through collective quests, shared learning tracks, and governance participation, the most consistent members become the most influential.

This isn't a loyalty programme. It's a living structure that gets stronger the more people show up.

DAO Governance

$DVT is designed to carry governance — a say in product direction, community initiatives and the future shape of DAOasis. Governance will be introduced in stages as the ecosystem matures.

Stake to Create

Community members with sufficient participation can propose and launch new quests, learning modules, and sanctuary experiences — bringing real creative input into the ecosystem they helped build.

Collective Decisions

From partner integrations to sanctuary locations and reward structures — the decisions that shape DAOasis are made with the community, not for it. Your consistency earns your seat at the table.
