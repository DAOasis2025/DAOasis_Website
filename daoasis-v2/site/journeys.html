Journeys are digital, inspired by real routes. You walk them through everyday life, wherever you are.

What a Journey is

Not a step counter. A way through.

Most virtual walks turn steps into a line on a map. A DAOasis Journey asks more of the day, and gives more back.

Movement carries you along the route. Sleep, breath and water decide when the next stage opens. What you learn and reflect on tells you the story of the place you are passing through. Your everyday actions are taking you somewhere.

How a Journey unfolds

Seven steps, then the next.

Every Journey follows the same shape, whether it crosses Thailand, climbs to Everest Base Camp or runs the length of Britain. Completing one does not end anything. It opens the next.

  1. 01ChoosePick a Journey that means something to you.
  2. 02LiveBuild healthy habits in ordinary days — move, rest, breathe, drink water, eat well, learn.
  3. 03ProgressEverything you do moves you through the route. Some stages ask you to slow down.
  4. 04DiscoverEach place has a story, a lesson and a question to sit with.
  5. 05ConnectWalk alongside the DAOasis community, with a shared marker on every route.
  6. 06ArriveReach the destination, and keep what it taught you.
  7. 07ContinueChoose your next Journey. DAOasis keeps going because you do.

Acclimatisation

Rest is part of the Journey.

On the real mountain, trekkers walk high and sleep low, and spend whole days not climbing at all. The body needs time to adapt, and the people who arrive well are the ones who give it that time.

The Everest Journey builds that in. At Namche Bazaar and Dingboche the route waits. Sleep, breathing, water and recovery build your readiness, and the next stage opens when you are ready — not when you push harder. Steps beyond a comfortable day do not carry you further.

Sometimes progress means rest, recovery, sleep, reflection, learning or simply breathing. How readiness is measured is still in design.
