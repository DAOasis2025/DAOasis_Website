01 · The opportunity

Information is not behaviour.

People have never had more wellness information, tracking or content, and consistency has not improved to match. Steps live in one app, sleep in another, learning somewhere else. Nothing accumulates, and nothing gives a person a reason to come back tomorrow.

The gap is sustained behaviour, which is a systems problem rather than a content problem. DAOasis is built as that system: daily habits become visible progress, progress is recognised, and the route leads somewhere real.

03 · How it works

Your habits become a Journey.

900Kilometres

Daily behaviour moves a marker along a real route, Bangkok to Phuket. A step count is a number; the same step placed on a route is progress toward somewhere. Bangkok to Phuket is the launch Journey, the first of a repeatable format: completing one opens the next.

04 · How it earns

Free to start. Membership to keep walking.

Every member walks the first stage of a Journey free, with all six inputs. Membership continues the route. Those who do not pay keep Basecamp: their check-in, the community and Reward Credits at the same rates. Prices are proposed and untested.

Membership Proposed
Every Journey, the full curriculum, insights and Circles. The primary, scalable line.
Education and cohort learning Planned
Six-week guided cohorts, sold separately. Members pay less.
Sanctuary experiences Partner test
Premium stays, paid in ordinary currency, never in token. Tested through a partner first.
Wellness and brand partnerships Exploratory
Practitioners, wearable brands, hospitality and employer-paid team seats.
Marketplace Longer term
Member-made courses and experiences, after $DVT and legal review.
No revenue from the token $DVT is not a revenue line, and nothing above has been earned or tested. Starting is free and needs no wallet.
