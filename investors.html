01 · The thesis

Why DAOasis. Why now.

Six markets that used to sit apart are converging on the same customer: preventative wellness, behavioural technology, digital learning, community platforms, experiential hospitality, and digital ownership. Each is large. None of them, on its own, solves the problem the customer actually has.

The problem is not access to wellness information. It is sustained behaviour — and sustained behaviour is a systems problem, not a content problem. DAOasis is being built as that system: one product that connects daily behaviour to visible progress, progress to recognition, and recognition to a real destination.

Large markets do not automatically create durable products. What makes this moment different is that the behavioural gap, the technology to close it, and consumer comfort with participation-based platforms have arrived together.

02 · The problem

Information is not behaviour.

People have never had more wellness information, more tracking, or more content. Consistency has not improved to match.

01
Attention is fully spoken for
Remote-first, digitally connected professionals — the exact demographic most exposed to burnout — are also the most fragmented. Routine breaks first, and it breaks quietly.
02
Wellness behaviour is measured in silos
Steps live in one app, sleep in another, learning somewhere else entirely. Nothing accumulates. Nothing compounds. A good week and a bad week look the same by Friday.
03
Tracking is not a reason to return
Most wellness products optimise for subscription conversion or content consumption. They record what a user did without giving them a reason to do it again tomorrow.
04
Progress has nowhere to go
Even where consistency is achieved, it stays abstract — a number, a streak, a graph. It does not convert into standing, ownership, or anything the user can hold.
Framing draws on the behavioural-engagement literature (NIH), which finds engagement strongest around goal tracking, feedback, rewards and social accountability — the four things fragmented tracking cannot deliver together.

04 · The differentiator

Your habits become a journey.

900Kilometres

Inside the Companion App, daily behaviour drives a marker along a real route through Thailand — Bangkok to Phuket. Steps are the engine. Breathing, hydration and sleep add distance on top. Six waypoints, each one unlocking as it is reached.

This is the mechanic that separates DAOasis from a tracker. A step count is a number. The same step, placed on a route, is progress toward somewhere — and the somewhere is not fictional. It is the site of the planned Sanctuary.

07 · Recognition

Participation becomes recognised.

DAOasis has two distinct things, and conflating them is the fastest way to misread the company. DRC is the everyday participation layer. $DVT is the on-chain layer. They are not two names for the same asset.

The separation is deliberate and it is structural. It lets DAOasis build real engagement mechanics now, for every user, without the legal and regulatory weight of a tradeable instrument sitting on top of a daily wellness habit.

DRC
DAOasis Reward Credits
Earned through meaningful participation — wellness routines, quests, learning, challenges, community activity, referrals and contribution. DRC lives inside the app, is subject to daily caps and streak multipliers, and is redeemable within the ecosystem.
What DRC is not Not a cryptocurrency. Not tradeable outside the app. Not a financial instrument, and not something to speculate on. It carries no monetary guarantee and no market price.
$DVT
DAOasis Value Token
The on-chain layer, carrying the deeper participation mechanics: marketplace, governance, staking, Stake to Create and ecosystem contribution. Fixed supply, single token — the earlier dual-token model was retired in favour of this.
What $DVT is not Not an investment product, not a store of value, and not offered here. DAOasis publishes no price, yield or return expectation of any kind. Launch follows completion of legal review.
Why a user never has to care The Companion App is designed to be fully usable with no wallet, no token and no crypto knowledge. DRC is available to every user by default. Anything to do with $DVT is opt-in, and sits at the far end of a journey that begins with going for a walk.

Conversion is a pathway, not a requirement

A member can participate for years, earn DRC continuously and redeem it inside the ecosystem without ever touching the on-chain layer. That is not a fallback — it is the design. Requiring the wallet would shrink the top of the funnel before it starts.

Which is why the sequencing is what it is

The Companion App and Reward Credits are being built first, because they carry no token-related legal exposure and unblock the entire daily engagement loop on their own. $DVT follows once its legal and structural framework is complete — never the other way round.

10 · Contributor economy

Don't just participate. Build.

In design · not yet operational

Most consumer wellness products have exactly one relationship available: customer. Stake to Create is the mechanism by which an aligned member can hold a second one — contributor.

The intent is that members who have demonstrated genuine participation can stake $DVT to put forward and deliver approved contributions to the ecosystem itself, rather than only consuming what the company produces.

01
Content and education
Curriculum modules, guided sessions and practitioner-led material inside the learning tracks.
02
Products and experiences
Offerings that reach the ecosystem through the marketplace rather than around it.
03
Events and community
Cohorts, challenges, local gatherings and the accountability structures around them.
04
Ecosystem initiatives
Partnerships, ambassador territories and projects that widen what DAOasis can offer.

Why this matters commercially: a contributor economy changes the cost curve of supply. Content, programming and community depth are the expensive parts of a wellness business. A model where the most engaged members can help produce them — with a stake behind the commitment — is structurally different from a company that must buy all of it.

11 · The physical layer

The digital journey leads somewhere real.

App → journey → participation → DRC → $DVT → Sanctuary. The chain only means something because the last link is physical. A wellness app whose reward is more app has a ceiling; one that terminates in a place a member can actually stand does not.

The Sanctuary is planned as a premium behavioural immersion environment — not a real estate thesis. Its purpose is to accelerate onboarding, deepen commitment, validate the methodology in person, and give the community a centre of gravity.

01
Phuket, planned for 2027
An established global wellness-tourism destination with mature hospitality infrastructure and strong international access — 7.6M+ visitors between January and July 2025, per Tourism Authority of Thailand data.
02
16 rooms. Adults only.
Designed around an experiential sweet spot of 10–15 guests. The capacity ceiling is physical and real, which is precisely what makes access to it worth competing for.
03
A seven-day rolling programme
No fixed cohort start date. Guests arrive on any day and join a rhythm already in motion — structured, but not regimented.
04
Asset-light by intent
Leased or partnership-based hospitality models rather than owned infrastructure, operated through a separate Thai entity so the digital ecosystem is never carrying hospitality risk.
See the Sanctuary
Planning assumptions — not validated The company's current sanctuary model assumes an average of US$700 per guest per day (5-day US$2,500 · 7-day US$3,200), 70% target annual occupancy, roughly 572 guests per year and estimated annual revenue above US$1.1M at an estimated 18–22% net margin after operating costs. These are internal planning assumptions carried over from the investor pack. They have not been validated against a signed lease, a named property, or local Phuket hospitality comparables, and should be treated as a model rather than a forecast.

13 · Community

Built to get better as more people arrive.

"Network effect" is claimed far more often than it is earned, so it is worth being specific about the mechanism here rather than asserting the label.

DAOasis strengthens with participation for one concrete reason: a meaningful share of what the ecosystem offers is produced by its members. Cohorts, challenges, check-ins and accountability only exist if people show up for each other. Learning depth, marketplace supply and community programming are intended to come substantially from contributors. And access to the constrained parts — Sanctuary cohorts, curated marketplace positions, ambassador territories — becomes more meaningful, not less, as more members qualify for it.

The honest counterpart: that mechanism only works if the caps stay real. The moment capacity is quietly loosened to chase revenue, the argument collapses into ordinary designed scarcity. Holding those limits is a discipline the company has to keep choosing.

19 · Long-term direction

Your progress should belong to you.

Today: DAOasis recognises participation through DRC and creates a pathway toward digital ownership through $DVT. That is the whole of it.

Where it could go: the same architecture that lets someone own a position in an ecosystem could, in time, let them own the record of what they have actually done — verified achievements, completed learning, credentials that travel with the person rather than sitting inside whichever app happened to record them.

That is the long-term digital sovereignty thesis, and it is the reason one of the two learning tracks is called Digital Sovereignty. It is also the part of this page furthest from being built.

Future exploration · not built
To be unambiguous DAOasis does not store health data on a blockchain, and has no product that does. Nothing described in this section exists today. It is a direction under exploration, subject to legal, regulatory and technical review, and DAOasis makes no medical, privacy or data-custody claims on the strength of it.
20 · The synthesis

Seven reasons this can work.

01
Behaviour first
The product earns its engagement through wellness, not through a reward. If the daily loop does not work on its own merits, nothing downstream of it matters — which is why it is being built first and alone.
02
Connected, not bundled
Six metrics feeding one journey is a different product from six metrics displayed side by side. The connection is the invention.
03
Accessible by design
No wallet, no token and no crypto literacy required to start. The addressable market is everyone who wants to build a habit, not everyone who already owns a wallet.
04
Real capacity, not manufactured scarcity
The Sanctuary has a finite number of rooms. Access to a genuinely constrained thing is worth holding in a way that a designed limit never is.
05
A physical destination
The digital journey terminates somewhere a member can stand. Very few wellness products have anywhere for progress to arrive.
06
Members can become builders
Stake to Create opens a second relationship beyond customer — and changes the cost curve of the expensive parts of a wellness business.
07
Sequenced, not rushed
Capital, product and legal readiness are gated against each other on purpose. DAOasis has to earn the right to introduce $DVT through traction and diligence rather than using it to fund the company into existence.
